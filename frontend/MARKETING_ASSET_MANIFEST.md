## 2. Production Asset Rules
1. **Zero Third-Party Copyright Infringement**: No Beacons logos, Beam mascots, or proprietary creator photographs are exposed in production.
2. **Dynamic React Components**: All floating cards, pricing tiers, and AI audit feeds are built as live, editable React components with real Tailwind tokens and Framer Motion transitions.
3. **Official Kreato Assets**: The official Kreato K-symbol logo (`/public/kreato-logo.png`) is preserved and prominently featured in navigation, hero, dark showcase, and authentication experiences.
